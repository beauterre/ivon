A little story for

Yvon

Something small, with a secret inside.

Dear Yvon,

When I wanted to give you something for your baptism, I started by imagining something quite elaborate.

I liked the idea of making something beautiful and unusual. Something that had clearly been made especially for you.

But the more I thought about it, the more I realised that there was another quality I wanted your gift to have.

I wanted it to be useful in a way that you might not understand until much later in life.

✦

So I changed my mind.

Instead of making something complicated, I chose something very simple.

Just your name.

Nothing flashy. Nothing that looks particularly expensive. Something that you can wear without announcing what it is worth.

At first glance, it looks almost like silver.

The little secret

It isn't silver.

It is white gold.

I chose white gold because I liked the way it looks — quiet, simple, and almost silver-like. But underneath that appearance is something rather more valuable.

That was deliberate.

Why?

I hope you will have a wonderful life, and I hope you will never need the reason I chose it this way.

But life does not always go according to plan.

There may one day be a difficult time when you need money quickly. Perhaps you will have an unexpected problem, or find yourself somewhere far from home, or simply need a little bit of help when there is nobody else to provide it.

If that ever happens, I want you to remember this:

This little piece of jewellery was meant to be worth something.

Not just because it is a present. Not just because it carries your name. But because I chose the material itself with that possibility in mind.

If you ever genuinely need to sell it, then sell it. You do not have to feel guilty about that.

The gift was never meant to be more important than you.

✦

And there is another part.

The back was deliberately left with enough room for something that isn't there yet.

Your birth date.

And your baptism date.

One day, those dates can be engraved on the back, so that the little piece carries not only your name, but also the beginning of your story.

So if you are reading this many years from now, perhaps you are wondering why your godparent gave you such a simple little necklace.

Now you know.

It was supposed to look simple.
It was supposed to be difficult to steal because nobody should have much reason to notice it.
And it was supposed to be there, quietly, if you ever really needed it.

Most of all, though, I hope that you wear it simply because you like it.

And I hope that when you are old enough to understand this little secret, you will also understand what was behind it:

Someone was thinking about you not only on the day you received it, but about the many years of your life that were still to come.

With love,
Your godparent
